# Arcania — Step-by-Step Walkthrough

> This walkthrough is written against the design bibles (`01-gdd.md`, `02-world-design.md`, `05-boss-bible.md`, `06-magic-system.md`, `07-narrative.md`). It covers the intended 1.0 critical path: 12 regions, 14 dual-purpose spells, 8 major bosses, and the three ending branches.
>
> **Note on names:** some regional sections in `02-world-design.md` use alternate names for spells (e.g., *Arc Step* for *Veil Step*, *Rootbind* for the vine-growth form of *Rune Anchor*, *Echo Sense* for *Ley Sight*). This guide uses the canonical 14-spell names from the GDD and magic-system doc so the loadout wheel stays consistent.

## Before You Start

### Default Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Move | A / D or ← / → | Left stick / D-pad |
| Jump | Space | A / Cross |
| Melee | J or Left Click | X / Square |
| Cast Spell | K or Right Click | Y / Triangle |
| Aim Spell | Mouse / IJKL | Right stick |
| Spell Wheel | Tab (hold) | LB (hold) |
| Quick Slots | 1–4 | D-pad |
| Veil Step / Dash | Shift | B / Circle |
| Interact | E | A (context) |
| Map | M | Select / View |

### Core Rules to Remember

- **Every spell does two jobs.** Combat use is usually obvious; exploration use opens the map. If you are stuck, look for the environmental clue matching your newest spell.
- **Overcast is clutch, not rotation.** Casting at zero mana costs HP. Three overcasts within 8 seconds inflicts **Silence** (no casting for 4 seconds).
- **Rest at every Focus Crucible / Waystone.** This restores HP and mana, saves progress, and (later) lets you fast travel.
- **Read telegraphs.** Amber = fast dodge. Crimson = heavy commit. Violet = grab/pull. Cyan = stagger/counter window. White = floor hazard.
- **Backtrack after each new spell.** The map is built on ability gates; new spells unlock shortcuts and secrets in earlier regions.

## Act I — Ashen Threshold & The Three Exits

### 1. Prologue — Awaken with the Ember Sigil

You start as **Elara Veilmark** in the Ashen Threshold with only your melee attack and **Ember Sigil**.

**What to do:**

1. Head east through the tutorial corridor. Light any unlit braziers with Ember Sigil — this is the first ability gate.
2. Speak with **Magister Corin** at the central plaza. This unlocks the hub **Waystone** (free) and the *Null-Rope Bind* quest.
3. Rest at the hub Waystone. After resting, the three regional gates open simultaneously.

**Tips:**

- Explore the Threshold thoroughly before leaving. Several secrets here require spells you do not yet have (Rootbind vines, Ley Sight hidden wall, Echo Sense memory chamber).
- Optional: rescue the **Hollow pup** in a burial niche; this affects merchant pricing later.

### 2. Choose Your First Region

After the hub opens, three exits are available in any order:

| Direction | Region | Reward | Why go first? |
|-----------|--------|--------|---------------|
| East | Whisperwood Hollow | **Veil Step**, path to **Rune Anchor** | Easiest traversal gate; teaches phase/dash combat |
| North | Sunken Catacombs | **Spirit Form** (via Kindling Ward path) / **Ley Sight** | Good for map secrets and hidden paths |
| West | Bleakfen Marsh | **Frost Bind** path, water puzzles | Larger region, more Essence, harder platforming |

**Recommended first-timer route:** Whisperwood Hollow → Sunken Catacombs → Bleakfen Marsh.

## Act II — Early Regions (Whisperwood / Catacombs / Bleakfen)

### 3. Whisperwood Hollow — Veil Step & the Root Warden

**Goal:** defeat the **Thornweft Matron** (mini-boss), acquire **Veil Step**, then defeat **The Root Warden** for **Rune Anchor**.

**Critical path:**

1. Forest Gate → Whisper Path → Thorn Hollow.
2. In **Heartwood Chamber**, interact with the vine-choked arch to acquire **Rootbind** (the vine-growth exploration form of Rune Anchor).
3. Use Rootbind to climb the Vine Lift into the upper canopy.
4. Cross Canopy Walk and reach **Heartwood Grove**.
5. Defeat **MB-01 Thornweft Matron**.

**Thornweft Matron strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Stay mobile. Destroy Thorn Pillars when safe — each destroyed pillar becomes a safe gap in Phase II's Canopy Rain.
- **Root Snare:** jump or Veil Step out of the violet ring; if snared, melee the exposed root bulb.
- **Phase II:** Arena shrinks. Stand under destroyed pillar gaps during Canopy Rain. Use Veil Step i-frames for Heartwood Pulse.
- Reward: path to Ironroot Depths.

6. After Matron, the **Arc Step** upgrade is granted (short blink through thin walls; counts as the Attuned form of Veil Step).
7. Continue through Ironroot Gate → Cart Tunnel → Anchor Tutorial.
8. Defeat **BOSS-01 The Root Warden**.

**Root Warden strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Avoid Anchor Pull (violet tether). When a Root Spear sticks in the ground, it is a T4 stagger window — melee it.
- **Cart Crush:** grapple to the ceiling anchor ring when the track glows white.
- **Phase II:** Warden merges with the wall. Use anchor rings to flank; he is only vulnerable from the side.
- **Phase III:** Mass Pull activates all rings. Counter-pull at the shaft edge to stagger him.
- Reward: **Rune Anchor** spell.

**After Whisperwood:**

- You now have: Ember Sigil, Veil Step / Arc Step, Rootbind, Rune Anchor.
- Backtrack to Ashen Threshold with Rootbind to open the east cliff shortcut.

### 4. Sunken Catacombs — Spirit Form & Ley Sight

**Goal:** defeat the **Reliquary Sentinel**, then the **Archivist Phantom** for **Ley Sight**.

**Critical path:**

1. Enter from Ashen Threshold north stair. Move through flooded corridors.
2. In the Urn Vault, defeat **MB-02 Reliquary Sentinel** or bypass it with Gleamflare (if you already have it from another route).
3. Acquire **Kindling Ward** from the Sentinel drop; this blocks fire/corruption damage and is the combat pre-requisite for **Spirit Form**.
4. Find the **Veilmark Urn Vault** (hidden behind a Gleamflare illusion) to acquire **Gleamflare** if you do not already have it.
5. Use the phantom stair to reach the **Archive of Echoes**.
6. Defeat **MB-05 Index Hydra** in the Central Catalog.
7. Enter the Library of Maps and defeat **BOSS-02 Archivist Phantom**.

**Archivist Phantom strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Page Blade is a three-slash combo; close the gap after the third slash. Misfile Blink is telegraphed — the real position leaves a faint crack.
- **False Aisle trap:** if the floor looks suspicious, probe with Ember Sigil or wait for Ley Sight after the fight.
- **Phase II:** Audit Seal marks a joint with cyan T4. Strike it for +15% damage.
- **Phase III:** Arena shelves collapse. Invisible platforms are real; fake platforms lack the faint cyan pre-glow.
- **CRITICAL:** When the Phantom offers you the Archivist position, **refuse**. Accepting triggers a fake-death trap unless you already used Ley Sight to reveal the false floor.
- Reward: **Ley Sight**.

**After Catacombs/Archive:**

- You now have: Spirit Form, Ley Sight.
- Backtrack everywhere with Ley Sight to reveal hidden walls, platforms, and secret rooms.

### 5. Bleakfen Marsh — Frost Bind

**Goal:** acquire **Frost Bind** and the **Tidecaller**-style water manipulation, then defeat **Miremother**.

**Critical path:**

1. Enter from Ashen Threshold west gate. Wade through shallow fen.
2. At the cult altar, acquire **Tidecaller** (raise/lower water levels).
3. Use Tidecaller to drain flooded passages and reach the Drowning Circle.
4. Defeat **MB-03 Miremother**.

**Miremother strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Jump Tentacle Sweeps; hit the tentacle base during T4 window.
- **Memory Lure:** safe-looking tiles are illusions. Probe with Ember or use Ley Sight.
- **Fen Pull:** Veil Step out, or Tidecaller (if acquired) to reverse the pull.
- **Phase II:** Sinkhole widens. Stay near the edge and strike the bulb when it surfaces.

5. Drain the central fen with Tidecaller to reveal **The Hollow Heart** secret zone (optional; see Optional Content).
6. Exit north through the cracked dam to **Obsidian Atelier** (requires Stoneheart, see Act III) or return to hub.

**After Bleakfen:**

- Recommended next: Lumineth Caverns (Weightless) or Obsidian Atelier (Stoneheart).

## Act III — Mid Regions (Lumineth / Archive / Skyfall / Atelier)

### 6. Lumineth Caverns — Weightless & Crystal Bridge

**Entry:** Whisperwood Rootbind vine elevator OR Bleakfen submerged root tunnel.

**Goal:** defeat **Prism Warden**, then **Crystal Leviathan** for **Crystal Bridge**.

**Critical path:**

1. Ascend the crystal shafts. Use Weightless updrafts to cross gravity wells.
2. Defeat **MB-04 Prism Warden** in the Crystal Spire Antechamber.
3. Enter the Glacial Rift and defeat **BOSS-03 Crystal Leviathan**.

**Crystal Leviathan strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Jump Tail Sweeps. Break crystal growth H1 to interrupt Ice Breath.
- **Phase II:** The arena scrolls. Use the trial **Crystal Bridge** casts to cross bridge gaps.
- **Phase III:** Vertical climb on the Leviathan's back. Hide behind growths during Prism Laser.
- Reward: **Crystal Bridge**.

### 7. Skyfall Ruins — Ley Anchor & Storm Lash

**Entry:** Lumineth updraft OR Archive shelf bridge.

**Goal:** defeat **Bridge Warden Kael**, then **The Fallen Magister / Starfall Regent** for **Storm Lash** and **Ley Anchor**.

**Critical path:**

1. Cross the floating debris. Stabilize skybridges with Ley Anchor panels.
2. Defeat **MB-06 Bridge Warden Kael** on the Collapsed Span.

**Kael strategy:**

- Drop to lower ledges to avoid Lance Thrust.
- Veil Step through Toll Bell shockwaves.
- In Phase II, destroy Lightning Rods immediately; this foreshadows Storm Lash.

3. Ascend the Regent's tower and defeat **BOSS-04 The Fallen Magister**.

**Fallen Magister strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Weave through Orb Volley. Exit the shadow before Levitation Crush lands.
- **Phase II:** Lure the Magister over conductive pools, then hit him with Storm Lash for a chain stun.
- **Phase III:** Hide at the conduit base during Engine Overload; Bulwark optional.
- Reward: **Storm Lash** and **Ley Anchor**.

**After Skyfall:**

- Ley Anchor unifies the Waystone network globally.
- Two exits open: Sanctum of Ash (up) and Undercrown Kingdom (down).

### 8. Obsidian Atelier — Stoneheart & Spirit Form (Major)

**Entry:** Bleakfen cracked dam (requires Stoneheart) OR long hub loop.

**Goal:** defeat **Anvil Ascendant**, then **Subject IX** for **Spirit Form**.

**Critical path:**

1. Navigate forge vents and molten channels. Kindling Ward reduces fire chip.
2. Defeat **MB-07 Anvil Ascendant**.

**Anvil Ascendant strategy:**

- Stand on anvil covers during Vent Flush.
- Parry the third hit of Hammer Triplet.
- Overheat the forge (environmental) to skip Phase I armor.
- Phase II afterimages are grey; the real body has a heartbeat ember.

3. Enter the Ectoplasm Containment wing and defeat **BOSS-05 Subject IX**.

**Subject IX strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Keep facing IX to prevent Observation Snap.
- Veil Step out of Cage Drop.
- **Phase II:** IX turns intangible. Use **Spirit Form** to pass through barriers and flank.
- **Phase III:** Possession grab — Spirit Form escapes and staggers.
- Reward: **Spirit Form**.

## Act IV — Late Regions (Sanctum of Ash / Undercrown Kingdom)

### 9. Sanctum of Ash — Arcane Bulwark

**Entry:** Skyfall burn-scar elevator (after Starfall Regent) OR Obsidian Atelier hidden tunnel.

**Goal:** defeat **Ember Confessor**, then **Cardinal of Ash** for **Arcane Bulwark**.

**Critical path:**

1. Kindling Ward is effectively mandatory for extended ash-fall rooms.
2. Optional but recommended: defeat **MB-08 Ember Confessor** in the Confession Ash-Pit.

**Ember Confessor strategy:**

- Answer confession questions **truthfully** to remove one attack pattern per honest answer and weaken Cardinal Voss later.
- Burn Ash Scrolls mid-flight with Ember Sigil.
- In Phase II, gaps in the Fire Screen correspond to honest answers.

3. Defeat **BOSS-06 Cardinal of Ash (Pyre Cardinal Voss)**.

**Cardinal Voss strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Block Ash Bolts with **Arcane Bulwark** to reflect them. Heavy Plate holds against Pyre Wave.
- Break Confession Chain anchors with melee.
- **Phase II:** Frontal Martyr Shield. Flank or face-tank with Bulwark Heavy Plate for stagger.
- **Phase III:** Ascendant Burn fills the arena. Stand inside Bulwark hex safe zones.
- Reward: **Arcane Bulwark** and **Void Mirror**.

### 10. Undercrown Kingdom — Shadow Blink & Frost Bind (Major)

**Entry:** Skyfall root chute OR Sanctum ash descent.

**Goal:** defeat the **Root Champion** phase and **The Undercrown** for **Frost Bind**.

**Critical path:**

1. Pass Captain Rell's blockade (bring a Thornbound Root-Sigil or Conclave Null-Seal).
2. Descend to the throne district. Open sealed vaults with Rift Tear (see below).
3. The major boss begins with **Phase 0 — Root Champion** parry tutorial.
4. Defeat **BOSS-07 The Undercrown**.

**The Undercrown strategy:**

- **Phase 0:** Parry Champion Cleave during the cyan T4 window.
- **Phase I:** Jump Crown Spikes. Use Frost Bind to root summoned Spore Knights.
- **Phase II:** Frost Mist slows and chips. Freeze it with Frost Bind, then shatter.
- Reward: **Frost Bind** and **Royal Ley Key**.

**Note on Rift Tear / Shadow Blink:** The design docs split this acquisition. In `02-world-design.md`, **Rift Tear** is obtained from the Undercrown vault; in `05-boss-bible.md` and `06-magic-system.md`, **Shadow Blink** is the acquired spell from the Obsidian Sanctum. Treat **Shadow Blink** as the canonical spell wheel entry; **Rift Tear** is the regional name for its planar-tear exploration effect.

## Act V — Somnium Rift & Ley Nexus

### 11. Somnium Rift — Temporal Echo & Unravel

**Entry:** Undercrown Spore Highway OR Archive dream catalog.

**Goal:** defeat **Mirror Elara**, then **Somnium Weaver** for **Temporal Echo**, and finally acquire **Unravel**.

**Critical path:**

1. Navigate dream-logic rooms. Geometry shifts on a timer; Ley Sight/Echo Sense reveals the next layout.
2. Defeat **MB-XX Mirror Elara** (Phase 0 integrated into the Weaver fight).

**Mirror Elara strategy:**

- She copies your last cast. Identify real vs mirror VFX (mirror is desaturated).
- Stay aggressive; she has lower HP than a full boss.

3. Defeat **BOSS-08 Somnium Weaver**.

**Somnium Weaver strategy:**

- **Phase I:** Thread Cut — Veil Step through. Dream Replay replays a previous boss attack; interrupt with Temporal Echo rewind.
- **Phase II:** Reverse Input warning appears for 60 frames. Pre-commit your movement.
- **Phase III:** Epoch Fracture — use Temporal Echo slow + strike.
- Reward: **Temporal Echo**.

4. Enter the dream-self chamber and acquire **Unravel**.

### 12. Ley Nexus — The Unbound Conclave

**Entry:** Break the forward seal in Somnium Rift with Unravel.

**Goal:** defeat the **Ley Guardian Triad** gauntlet, then **BOSS-FINAL The Unbound Conclave**.

**Prerequisites for the final boss:**

- All 14 spells acquired.
- Recommended: 11+ of 14 spells mastered; all 8 Focus Shards; Ley Navigator or similar map-reveal relic.

**Ley Guardian Triad strategy:**

- **Static Warden:** Never suppresses Ember Sigil. Dodge Null Blade; Veil Step through Suppress Pulse; stay at long range during Static Burst.
- **Weave Sentinel:** Element cycles every 20 seconds. Counter with the opposite spell type. In Phase II, use Unravel to strip the dual-element shield.

**The Unbound Conclave strategy:**

- **Phase I — Verdict:** Reflect Edict Beam with Bulwark. Veil Step out of Excision Circle; use Rune Anchor to pull adds into the circle.
- **Phase II — Static Chorus:** Use Temporal Echo to rewind the spell-suppress effect. Spirit Form through Static Merge. Region Barrage cycles through previous boss patterns — apply the counter you learned for each.
- **Phase III — Thread Choice:** World Thread is granted as a trial at the phase start. Use Thread Patch to block Thread Rip and the Instakill Weave. Crystal Bridge saves you from Platform Unstitch if Thread is on cooldown.
- At ~10% HP, the ending choice appears. See [The Three Endings](#the-three-endings).

## The Three Endings

The ending is determined by faction sympathy, Memory Shards collected, and your choice at the end of the Unbound Conclave fight. If you defeat the boss without choosing, the game defaults to whichever ending you have the highest sympathy for.

### Ending A — Repair the Weave (Canon / "Good")

- **Requirements:** Thornbound sympathy ≥ 2 OR Archive Keeper sympathy ≥ 2; Memory Shards 1, 3, 5, 6 collected; Last Page quest completed (recommended).
- **Action:** Cast **World Thread** to stitch the Unbound back into the Weave.
- **Result:** Magic survives but is diminished. Elara becomes a neutral Walker between factions. Post-credits: Sister Maelis records the true account.

### Ending B — Sever the Weave (Bittersweet)

- **Requirements:** Undercrown sympathy ≥ 2 OR Conclave sympathy ≥ 1; Sovereign Echo audience passed; Memory Shard 6.
- **Action:** Cast **Unravel** + **Storm Lash** to cut the static free.
- **Result:** Magic stabilizes, but the Unbound (and the Unwritten Child) are destroyed. Elara keeps full power but is emotionally isolated.

### Ending C — Join the Unbound (Secret / "Bad")

- **Requirements:** Unbound sympathy ≥ 3; Unwritten Child quest complete; NOT maximum Conclave sympathy.
- **Action:** Choose to **do nothing / accept** for 10 seconds during the ending prompt.
- **Result:** Elara dissolves into static. The Weave remains torn. Unlocks **Hollow Walker** mode for NG+.

## Closing Tips

- **If lost:** Open the map (`M`). Unexplored rooms are fog; visited rooms show connections. Ley Navigator relic marks secrets.
- **If under-leveled:** Farm Essence near a Crucible, buy consumables from Peddler Ash, or hunt optional elites for relics.
- **If stuck on a boss:** Equip a defensive relic (Rune-Stitched Hem, Flickering Ash) and focus on learning one pattern per attempt. No main-path boss requires Overcast.
- **For 100% completion:** Finish all five secret zones, collect all Memory Shards, and complete the Unwritten Page before the final boss to unlock the true ending branch.

*Good luck, Anchor. Hold the thread.*
